Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 3:1 Queensland Transport logbook credit work? ▼

Under Queensland Transport rules, every 1 hour of professional driving instruction with an accredited trainer counts as 3 hours in your logbook (up to a maximum of 10 structured daytime hours, yielding 30 logbook credit hours). You save 20 hours of driving while receiving top-tier road safety pedagogy.

How do I complete my booking and payment? ▼

ClutchEd is powered by the Clutch App. When you click "Book Lesson", you are directed straight to ClutchEd's portal inside the Clutch app (/s/clutched). You can select your instructor, transmission, and exact calendar time slot, and securely pay via credit card or Apple/Google Pay.

Where can I be picked up and dropped off? ▼

We provide door-to-door pickup and drop-off across all suburbs in Cooroy, Gympie, and the Mary Valley (including Imbil, Kandanga, Amamoor, Pomona, and Cooran). You can also request pickup from your school (e.g. Gympie State High, Noosa District High) or workplace.

These advanced programs are currently in active curriculum development and facility setup. You can join the early-access waitlist by emailing info@clutched.au to receive priority booking notifications and discounted inaugural slots.
